Privacy policy

Paru’s Bike Care Center Tracker · Last updated 7 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy explains how the Paru’s Bike Care Center Tracker (“the tracker”) handles information. The tracker is a program that runs on the shop owner’s own computer. Read what it does.

Information the tracker keeps

The tracker stores employee names, contact details, roles, salaries, attendance and payment records, and the sign-in details of the people who use it. All of this is stored in files on the owner’s computer, in a folder the owner chooses. The developer of the tracker does not receive, see or store any of it.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If the owner connects Google Drive, the tracker asks Google for one permission: access to files and folders that the tracker itself creates (the “drive.file” permission). It cannot read, list or change any other file in the owner’s Drive.

  • The tracker uploads copies of its employee, attendance, payment and settings files to a folder it creates in the owner’s Drive.
  • Sign-in details for the tracker’s users are never uploaded.
  • The sign-in token Google issues is kept only on the owner’s computer.
  • The information uploaded goes to the owner’s Google account and nowhere else. It is not shared with the developer or any other party, and it is not used for advertising.

The tracker’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Press “Disconnect” in the tracker at any time to stop backups.
  • Remove the tracker’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the backup folder from Google Drive, or delete the tracker’s data folder from the computer, to remove the records.
